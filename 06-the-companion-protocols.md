# The Companion Protocols

This repository documents the work-shape layer. The voice channel and the artisanal glyphs alphabet are the surface layers. Three protocols. One body of work. They cohere.

---

## ◊ Voice as Channel

[github.com/asastuai/voice-as-channel](https://github.com/asastuai/voice-as-channel)

How the AI was taught to write in the author's voice. Six short documents covering the problem, the extraction, the canon (verbal patterns + visual architecture), the application across registers, the closing signature, the artisanal-glyph alphabet.

The voice channel is what makes the outputs of the working method feel authored.

The working method without the voice channel produces well-shaped work that does not have a hand. The voice channel without the working method produces well-styled emptiness.

Read both. Adopt either or both. They are independent in principle and complementary in practice.

---

## What the three protocols do together

The working method (this repo) is what shapes the work.

The voice channel is what shapes the prose.

The artisanal-glyph alphabet (a sub-document of the voice channel, but worth naming separately) is what shapes the typographic surface.

When all three are operating, an output produced by the AI for the author has:

- **The right scope.** The working method tells the AI what to do, what to skip, what to defer.
- **The right tone.** The voice channel tells the AI how the prose should sound.
- **The right marks.** The glyph alphabet tells the AI which typographic accents are part of the author's signature surface.

When any of the three is missing, a layer of authentication is missing from the output. The reader may not be able to articulate what is off, but something will be.

## ❖ How a future author would adopt these

The three protocols are independent. An author adopting any one of them is not committing to the other two.

But the gains compound when all three are in place.

### Adopt the working method first

The working method is the most generic. It is about how to use AI tools to do research-and-build work in a way that produces public artifacts that survive scrutiny. The patterns in the eight phases are adaptable to most domains.

An author adopting the working method without yet adopting the voice channel can still produce defensible work. The work will not feel authored, but it will be honest.

### Adopt the voice channel second

Once the working method is producing artifacts, the voice channel is what gives those artifacts authorial fingerprint. The fingerprint matters most when the artifacts go public — research papers, blog posts, cold emails, application essays.

An author adopting the voice channel without the working method can produce well-voiced outputs that are nevertheless thin in substance. The voice is the surface; the method is the substance.

### Adopt the glyph alphabet third

The glyph alphabet is the smallest of the three. It is also the most stylistic. An author with a voice channel but no glyph alphabet still has a clear authorial fingerprint. Adding glyphs is a refinement, not a foundation.

The recommendation: do not adopt the glyph alphabet first. The discipline of choosing two or three glyphs and using them consistently is harder than it sounds. It is best earned after the voice channel is stable.

## ◊ A note on adapting these to a different author

The patterns in these three protocols are mine. They are extracted from how *i* think and write and ship.

Another author adapting them will end up with different patterns. That is correct. The contract is the practice of extraction and application, not the specific patterns extracted.

A non-exhaustive list of things that will be different for a different author:

- The voice canon will have different verbal patterns. Different em-dash policy. Different connector preferences. Different sentence-shape defaults.
- The visual architecture may not be Benedetti-shaped. It might be Borges-shaped. It might be neither.
- The glyph alphabet will have different glyphs. The signature will be different.
- The eight phases may collapse into six, or expand into ten. The author's specific stack will dictate.

The shape of the protocol is portable. The contents are not.

If you are reading this with intent to adopt, do not copy. Extract. Apply. Document. Iterate.

That is the contract.
